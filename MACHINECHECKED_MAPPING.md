# Navier–Stokes paper ↔ Lean: the audit's mapping

This table maps each numbered statement of *Finite time blowup for Navier–Stokes* ([PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/32d9f210-8b73-45e0-91bc-82a30aef8a9a/navier-stokes.pdf), 166 pages, 8 September 2026) to its counterpart in this repository at commit `f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd`. It is the auditor's reading of the paper and the Lean sources, made without a build; what Lean establishes about each declaration is the audit's (`machinechecked check`), and how far each declaration states the paper's claim is a reviewer's judgement, recorded on the Review page.

- **Counterpart**: the declaration states the paper's claim, up to harmless reformulation.
- **Partial**: it states part of the claim, a special case, or a variant; *Differences* says which.
- **None**: no counterpart found; *Why* says what was searched.
- *In closure*: the module is imported by `NavierStokes.ComparatorSolution`, the headline theorems' module.
- Statements read with low confidence, and those with no counterpart, are not linked in the audit.

Statement numbers cited in the Lean docstrings follow an earlier version of the paper; every mapping here was made by content.
